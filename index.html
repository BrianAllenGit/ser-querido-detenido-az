Can't reach someone and ICE may have them? Four steps, in the words of the official sources.

Important

This page gives general information and links to official sources. It is not legal advice and it cannot tell you what will happen in your family member's case. It does not save or collect any information about you. Links take you to other sites.

Four steps

  • Step 1. Find them
  • Step 2. Their court case
  • Step 3. Free legal help in Arizona
  • Step 4. Your family

Before you start

Gather this before you search. What ICE asks for is quoted in step 1.

  • Full name, as it appears on their papers
  • A-Number, if you have it
  • Country of birth
  • Date of birth, if you know it

To write on paper, print the card. This page does not keep anything you write on paper.

Write here

  • Full name
  • A-Number
  • Country of birth
  • Date of birth
  • Facility where they are (the locator says)
  • Facility phone
  • Lawyer or legal aid group
  • Who is caring for the children
