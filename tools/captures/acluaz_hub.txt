People who are immigrants are part of our families, communities, workplaces and places of worship. Like many of us, they join the long Arizona tradition of coming here in search of a better life and the freedom and opportunity we offer. No matter what someone looks like, sounds like, or what their immigration status may be, they should feel safe in their own communities.

The Trump Administration and too many of Arizona’s elected officials are undermining our shared values and threatening to violate our civil and constitutional rights. The ACLU of Arizona will always fight for the rights and dignity of immigrants and their families, and we need you with us.
