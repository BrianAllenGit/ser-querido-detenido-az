Immigrants: Know Your Rights

Last updated: August 13, 2025 04:45 pm

Share this page
Share on Facebook
Post
Copy

Regardless of your immigration status, you have guaranteed rights under the Constitution. Know your rights as an immigrant if you are approached by law enforcement – including local and state police, ICE (Immigration and Customs Enforcement), and Border Patrol.

This information is not intended as legal advice. Consult with an attorney if you have specific questions about your situation.
